# Project preferences

- Do not add decorative numbering anywhere in the frontend. Navigation labels, page headings, cards, section labels, coaching notes and branding must not use ordinal prefixes such as 01 or 02, numbered badges, or edition counters.
- Keep meaningful football information such as scores, budgets, ratings, league positions, fixture rounds and pagination.
- Keep provider credentials server-side and out of source control. The local `data/` folder contains user-supplied provider scripts/samples and is ignored by Git; inspect it without printing secrets or running unreviewed scripts.
- Keep previews local. Do not push or deploy without the user's explicit approval.
